Private Corrector settings

The extension opens claude.ai with your selected text and the instructions for the action. It uses your normal Claude account (Free, Pro or Max): no API key, no extra cost. Make sure you are signed in to claude.ai in Firefox.

Correct, Reformulate and Academic always keep the language of the text.
